## Notes on key numbers (for critique cross-check)
- N=791 professionals; 550 solutions/observations; 2×2 design.
- Quality over solo baseline (Table 2, [p.10–11]): Team No-AI +0.245 SD (p<0.05); Individual+AI +0.373 SD (p<0.01); Team+AI +0.392 SD (p<0.01); Team+AI vs Team No-AI n.s. (p=0.242).
